# Stock logos

Official marks of the stock tokens listed on the market pages (`/stocks`, `/swap`, `/dark-pool`). One PNG per token in
`public/stocks/<token address>.png`, 256x256, the mark trimmed and centred with about 10% padding on a transparent
background (CleanSpark and USA Rare Earth are the issuers' own square tiles). Registry: `lib/markets/logos.ts`. A market
whose token has no file here is not listed. All marks are trademarks of their owners.

The 36 files were converted on 2026-11-08 (ImageMagick, trim, resize to 208 px, pad to 256 px, PNG32) from the sources
in the table below (checked on 2026-11-01). NVIDIA uses the eye symbol only, cropped from the same Commons file, which
reads at small sizes. Robinhood's generic feather (`logoUrl` of its asset list) is never used.

On 2026-11-08 the fair-use and avatar files were reviewed against the issuers' own press and brand kits: Nebius (NBIS)
and Circle (CRCL) now use their brand-kit files, TSMC (TSM) and Invesco QQQ (QQQ) the logo files on their own sites
(neither publishes a logo kit). Alibaba (BABA) keeps the English Wikipedia file: its own media library restricts use to
accredited media.

**Hidden (owner decision, 2026-11-08: hide for now):** CRCL and BABA are not listed on Stocks, Private Swap or
Dark Pool until the issuer gives permission. Their registry entries are flagged `hidden` with the reason, their PNG files
are removed from `public/stocks/`, and no placeholder is shown. To list one again: get the issuer's written permission,
add its file back, and clear `hidden` in `lib/markets/logos.ts`. The other 34 stocks stay listed. Token addresses and Chainlink feeds
are the 36 stock tokens with a feed on 4663 (read on chain 2026-10-31).
